# A starter list of packages from Current RMS's past quotes

Research date: 8 November 2026, asked for by Colly ("Can we analyse the
previous quotes from current RMS to try find a pattern for creating a list
of these templates to start off?"). Nothing is loaded yet; this page is for
choosing. The list is also kept as data in
[package-starter-list.json](package-starter-list.json), ready to load the
packages Colly ticks.

## Recommendation

**Start with 21 of the 43.** The history shows a handful of
kit sets the company sends out again and again, sized in a few steps. The
ones worth loading first:

1. **The PA sizes**: Speech PA small and large, Party PA (YP10 + V subs),
   AL90 system, Y8 hang, V-Series hang, J-Series main stage. Every PA
   quote starts from one of these.
2. **The add-ons that always travel together**: Monitor wedges (4 and 8),
   In-ears, Axient radio mics, Stage boxes, Socapex run, DJ booth.
3. **The cable packs in two sizes**, power, speaker and XLR: half of all
   audio quotes list these cables one by one, the other half write "Cable
   package" or leave them off (see below), so a pack saves the most typing.
4. **Decor LX at €1,800 a week**, the one package RMS clearly charged as a
   single line with the same contents every time.

Leave for Colly to judge: the lighting rigs (the patterns there are weak),
the client packages (IPSC at Molesworth Street is very regular; NAMA less
so), and the mic packages, which can't be built properly until mics and DI
boxes are on the stock list.

## What was looked at

Colly's Current RMS export of 27 October 2026 (the same read the app's
Past quotes uses, ADR 0092): 1,072 quotes from April 2021 to 2027, 27,292
lines, of which 15,577 are stock products on 912 quotes, 6,592 are typed
lines and 661 are accessories. All of it was read; nothing in the app or
in Current RMS was changed. Quotes that never went ahead were kept in,
because what was quoted is what the office reaches for; about seven in ten
of the PA quotes went ahead either way.

The method, in short:

- **Which products go out together.** For every pair of products, how
  often they sit on the same quote compared with chance; then products
  grouped where they keep turning up together. That found the tight sets
  (Axient receiver, antennas and SM58s are on the same quote nine times in
  ten; IEM transmitters and beltpacks every time; J8, J12, J sub and fly
  frames together on over nine in ten J-Series quotes).
- **How big, in steps.** PA quotes split into four kinds by their main
  speaker: speech PA (point source, no subs), point source with subs,
  medium arrays (AL90, Y8, V, Q1) and J-Series. The quantities in each
  package are what those quotes most often had.
- **Typed package lines.** 609 typed lines are called a package, pack or
  kit ("Mic package" 140, "Mic / DI package" 54, "Cable package" 40,
  "Decor LX package" 22, "SDI package" 15, "Festival mic package" 14...).
  Most have no description, so they show the names the office uses and the
  price, not the contents.
- **Clients and venues.** For each client with ten or more quotes, the
  kit on at least half of them.

## What the history says

- **The office already thinks in sizes.** YP10s go out in twos (36) and
  fours (33) for speech; V subs in twos (71) and fours (41) under a party
  PA; M4 wedges in fours (76), twos (48), sixes, eights and twelves. A
  "small" and a "large" of each, as separate packages, covers most of it.
  (The quoting thread confirmed a size is just another package.)
- **The amp follows the speaker.** D20s go with YP10, E3 and Max 12
  (85-90%); D80s with J-Series and the arrays (85%); D40s with AL90 and
  wedges. Packages carry the amps their speakers need, not the monitors'.
- **Cables are the most typed and the least consistent.** Of 541 quotes
  with D&B amps, 317 list cables product by product, 71 write a typed
  "Cable package" (usually €100 a week, "complete" €300), and 186 list no
  cables at all. Typed cable packages are growing: 20 of 71 are from 2026.
  The two cable sizes below are what the itemised quotes most often held.
- **Mic packages are words, not kit.** "Mic package" is on 235 quotes,
  three in four at €0, almost never with contents ("As per spec"). Only
  one wired mic, the Shure SM 58, is on the stock list, with no DI boxes,
  57s or drum mics. So a mic package can only be its words and a stand
  case today; the proper fix is adding the mics and DIs to the stock list,
  then a package that holds them.
- **Some packages are prices, not kit.** "Decor LX package" was always
  €1,600 to €2,000 a week with the same contents. "DJ kit" was €400 to €560
  with three CDJs and a DJM. "Ops kit" €150 and "Audio package" €2,000
  have no contents written down at all.
- **Lighting is the least regular.** Lighting fixtures go out together far
  less than audio does: the strongest set (Tiger Touch, SGM P5s, Mega
  Pointes) is together on about a third of the quotes with the desk. The
  lighting packages here are suggestions to check, not patterns.
- **Some kit has moved on.** Q subs were on 101 quotes but only 8 since
  2025, and the Q1 hang only once since 2025, so the Q1 hang is left out
  and the Q-sub party PA is marked older.
- **One client books the same kit every time.** IPSC at Molesworth
  Street: 18 quotes from November 2023 to February 2026 with almost the
  same 22 products, most at the same quantity every time. NAMA's events
  are steady too, a speech PA with six radio mics.

price" is only set where RMS charged the set as one line; elsewhere it's
none, so the package goes on as separate lines priced from the price list
(the shape the quoting thread is building). The rate is Daily wherever
there is no one-line price, since each line then takes its product's own
rule.

  (SDI converters, reels). Worth making by hand if the office uses them.
